Vice Coast Cover Studio turns your photo and a caption into GTA-style multi-panel cover art, entirely offline in the browser. Pick one of seven layouts — a nine-panel cover grid, hero-and-three, four-panel grid, full bleed, tilted snapshots, triptych or story — grade it in one of six sunset and neon grades, set the bold title lettering, and export PNGs in 1080 × 1080, 1080 × 1350, 1920 × 1080 and 1080 × 1920 with one click. Every panel keeps its photo's face inside the frame, panning never reveals a white strip, and the palm-sun medallion sits inside the title band, clear of the badge ribbon.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in your browser. Load a sample photo or upload your own, choose a layout and grade, edit the title, and export.
